# Naijaclips – African Entertainment & Video Streaming Platform

Naijaclips is a modern Nigerian video-sharing, entertainment and streaming platform built to connect African creators, entertainers and audiences worldwide.

### Core Features

1. **Video Streaming**
   - Watch movies, music videos and entertainment content.
   - HD video playback.
   - Video categories and recommendations.
   - Video search and discovery.

2. **African Movies**
   - Nollywood movies.
   - African films and documentaries.
   - Movie trailers and previews.
   - Movie details and descriptions.

3. **YouTube Integration**
   - Discover YouTube videos.
   - Display video thumbnails.
   - Watch supported YouTube content.

4. **User Accounts**
   - User registration and login.
   - Secure authentication.
   - User profiles.
   - Password recovery.

5. **Video Sharing**
   - Upload and share videos.
   - Like and comment.
   - Share videos with friends.
   - Creator profiles.

6. **Admin Dashboard**
   - Manage users.
   - Manage videos and movies.
   - Moderate uploaded content.
   - Monitor platform activity.

7. **Additional Features**
   - Mobile-friendly design.
   - Search and filtering.
   - Trending videos.
   - Watch history and favourites.
   - Notifications.
   - Secure database.
   - Cloudflare deployment.
   - Video storage and delivery.

### Mission

To become a leading African entertainment destination where people can discover, watch, upload and share African content from anywhere in the world.
